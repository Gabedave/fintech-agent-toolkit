A complete structured result is scored once even after a later CLI failure. Raw provider diagnostics are excluded. Disposable installations preserve ordinary security policy and rules without forced tool grants. This removes separate successful-run recapture and policy-bypassing runner setup; it does not claim measured token or latency savings.
