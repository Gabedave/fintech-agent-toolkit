# Resume and decision record

## Resume capsule

- Outcome and checkable finish predicate
- Explicitly authorized and prohibited actions
- Repository, worktree, branch, base, and current commit
- Completed evidence and known failures
- Current design or root-cause model
- Highest-risk unknown and next safe action
- External waits or decisions required from a human

## Decision entry

Use one compact row or bullet per consequential decision:

`time | phase | decision or hypothesis | why | evidence | result | keep/revise/discard`

Use evidence pointers to code, checks, measurements, or authorized team records rather than long copied output. An unverified result stays unverified. One row or bullet covers one consequential decision or checkpoint; a TSV table is optional, not a requirement.

Records must remain understandable without transcript access. Refresh the capsule's current facts, but append corrections or superseding entries to the decision trail rather than rewriting earlier choices. Keep failed directions that shaped the outcome. If sensitive material was captured accidentally, remove it rather than preserving it for append-only purity, and note the redaction without repeating the data.
