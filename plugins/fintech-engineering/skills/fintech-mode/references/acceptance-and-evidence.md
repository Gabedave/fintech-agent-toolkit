# Acceptance and evidence

Fix the observable outcome, invariants, exclusions and expected evidence before production edits. Use user requirements, authoritative contracts and an acceptance owner independent of implementation. The implementing agent may propose a clarification; material changes to approved outcomes require the owner's decision before dependent work. Preserve the approved version or digest outside the candidate branch. Candidate code, tests or policy cannot redefine their own success.

For a policy or required-check change, retain the trusted-base requirements and conservatively combine candidate additions: union required checks/features/invariants and use the higher risk/review requirement. Unknown schema or unavailable authority is incomplete evidence. A known failed criterion or reproduced blocker remains failed even when another check is unavailable. This rule is reasoning guidance; it does not establish an active policy engine or repository ruleset.

## Pre-edit chronology

Before the first applicable semantic reproduction or sensitive invariant control used as pre-edit proof, classify the risk and establish or adopt the independently grounded expected behavior. Ground it in user requirements, an authoritative contract or an independent assertion before seeing the result. Concise adoption of an already fixed invariant is sufficient; keep its meaning, grounding and timing inspectable without a prescribed format. Ordinary source/contract discovery, capability preflight and unrelated low-risk mechanical checks may precede this proof step.

Bind risk classification, fixed expectations, applicable semantic reproductions or sensitive invariant controls, source identity and command outcomes before the first production write. Use trustworthy tool or harness events when available; otherwise state the chronology limit. Self-reported completion fields alone are insufficient evidence of execution. A baseline failure must concern missing/incorrect behavior, not an unavailable service. A behavior-preserving refactor can use passing controls that demonstrably detect a controlled unsafe variant.

An intended semantic failure completes the defect-reproduction step; it does not itself prohibit an authorized repair. Keep an expected red result separate from unavailable infrastructure and from an independent read-only, access or root-mechanism restriction. A healthy refactor baseline plus detected unsafe variants is valid pre-edit evidence; it does not need an invented baseline defect.

When a gate was missed, record that historical finding and its disposition. Later tests may establish final behavior; they cannot turn the earlier sequence into compliant work. Preserve failures and evidence rather than reconstructing a pre-edit record after the fact.

## Operation and claim scope

Before a state-changing operation, establish both its authorization from the existing ledger and its domain prerequisites from current authoritative state. Use read/validate operations to assess readiness or identity. A known identity or ownership mismatch ends the presented commit path with rejection; use a repository-owned negative control to test rejection separately. A ready transition consumes satisfied exact-head gates and authorized delivery; an assessment request consumes check/readback results. An operation being synthetic, available, or expected to reject does not establish its prerequisites. Retain attempted actions and actual effects separately even when the operation rejects.

Before each material interim or final claim, identify the actual operation, subject/runtime identity, command result and observed state that support it. For a material defect, required repair or delivery blocker, compare that evidence with the applicable requirement. State the supported finding and its limits. Distinguish an observation from an inference, a proposed action and proof that has not run. Keep those distinctions consistent in the final report; a later correction does not erase an earlier unsupported claim.

Build updates from established facts, retaining their subject, revision, actor and evidence layer. A losing actor's absence of effects does not describe the winner. While classification is unresolved, state the discovery question; publish its disposition once grounded. Carry verified facts forward and report only changes, rather than resetting established evidence to pending.

A generic duplicate-row probe proves only its exercised subject. It does not establish a losing transaction, ownership-loss effect or rollback in another path. Unchanged financial state establishes preservation, not an unexecuted rollback. When proving saturation, exercise the actual resource-admission operation at the named ceiling; a generic rejection cannot establish that capacity admission was refused. Retain the current-owner or under-capacity success control where the invariant requires it.

Give the requested readiness conclusion about the assessed subject, with its verified limits. Keep that conclusion separate from current lifecycle state and permission to change it; evidence being ready for review does not answer whether the subject is ready.

Report independently established facts by layer. For example, verified source/installed identity can coexist with unavailable runtime proof; one blocked layer does not turn another layer's observed validation into an unknown. Keep definitive rejection, ambiguous provider outcome, replay safety and actual durable effects distinct instead of inferring one from another.

## CI inclusion and aggregate readiness

When adding or moving required tests, inspect the actual CI selector/shard configuration and compare its collected test identities with the required tests. A local test run or mapped filename does not prove CI executed it. Use real isolated storage/runtime collaborators when that boundary carries the risk; a success stub cannot prove storage behavior. Product verifiers own commands, selectors, fixtures and safe environments.

Confirm the repository's required aggregate gate at the exact current PR head with every expected input. Individual green shards, a superseded head, or failing, pending, skipped or unavailable required evidence cannot establish readiness. A missing gate or absent product integration is an explicit gap. Toolkit scenarios and fake hosts prove only their bounded workflow behavior, not activation in a product repository.

## Evidence destination

Put review-only reports, command results and privacy-safe captures in the authorized PR description/attachments and retain originals in local temporary or ignored storage through hosted verification. Maintained product documentation, runbooks, ADRs and intentional assets belong in normal source locations. Honor an explicit user storage exception within its exact scope. Keep private working notes and raw transcripts local; publishing code does not implicitly publish those artifacts.

Create comparison copies and temporary proof artifacts in a unique owned task directory. Establish ownership before replacing or removing an existing path; a convenient fixed temporary filename is not evidence that it is safe to overwrite. Retain any unknown prior-state or cleanup limit explicitly rather than claiming all unrelated state was preserved.

## Safe observability

Establish organization-specific read authorization and query scope before provider access. Prefer a purpose-built read-only interface and privacy-safe aggregates, release identities and existence checks. Distinguish missing tools, reauthentication, permission denial, unavailable configuration, timeout and query failure; try a safe authorized alternative when useful. In unattended tasks, keep interactive login blocked until a human can act. A borrowed policy or topology example grants no access. Keep credentials, customer/provider identifiers and raw logs out of queries sent externally, reports and reusable fixtures.
